# Specialization — Sorcerer

Read this when a slice touches the Sorcerer's starter or perks.

Source: the perks in `src/data/specializations.ts`; the starter in `src/data/species/starters.ts`
and `src/data/traits/starters.ts`. The rules every spec follows (perk points, the 1000-point tree,
refunds, inert perks): `spec/progression.md` "Perks", "Perk points" and "Spending perk
points".

## Identity

The affinity **rule-breaker**: a versatile caster who equips spells across affinities and is
rewarded for gem *diversity*, not raw power. All content stays reachable by every spec; a spec
changes *how* you play.

## Starter: Glyphmoth Seer

Wit, high Intelligence; the name is a placeholder. Trait *Arcane Surge*: an **innate spell**,
Arcane Bolt, dealing 100% of its Intelligence (not a gem: un-upgradeable, no affinity gate, an
extra slot before the gem slots, kept through fusion), plus a 50% chance at the end of its turn to
cast a random equipped spell.

## Perks

Twelve perks; at max levels they cost exactly 1000 points. The Sorcerer is **gem-centric**, and the
gem-equip perks (the five Masteries, Arcane Shields, Arcane Versatility and True Wit, 600 points)
are inert: they need Phase 8's gem economy. The live ones (Echo, Clear Mind, Arcane Might and Spell
Focus, 400 points) are what the seed's points buy (at most 300, from three bosses). Before Phase 8
the Sorcerer is a one-caster-plus-boosts spec, thinner than the Brute and the Shieldbarer: an
accepted trade-off.

### Echo

1 level × 100 points. Your creatures Cast spells an additional time.

### Wit Mastery

1 level × 60 points. Your creatures can equip Wit spell gems regardless of affinity. Inert (needs
Phase 8).

### Violence Mastery

1 level × 60 points. Equip Violence gems regardless of affinity. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### Endurance Mastery

1 level × 60 points. Equip Endurance gems regardless of affinity. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### Vitality Mastery

1 level × 60 points. Equip Vitality gems regardless of affinity. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### Instinct Mastery

1 level × 60 points. Equip Instinct gems regardless of affinity. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### Clear Mind

1 level × 100 points. Your creatures are immune to the *effect* of Silenced: it still lands and
still counts for any "target is Silenced" payoff (a creature can take "+damage to Silenced"), but
they cast freely.

### Arcane Might

50 levels × 2 points. +1% Intelligence per level.

### Arcane Shields

10 levels × 10 points. −1% damage taken per level (cap 80%) for each equipped gem **not** of the
creature's affinity. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### Arcane Versatility

10 levels × 10 points. +1% damage per level for each equipped gem **not** of the creature's
affinity. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### True Wit

5 levels × 20 points. +1 spell gem slot per level. Inert (needs Phase 8).

### Spell Focus

100 levels × 1 point. +1% spell damage per level.
