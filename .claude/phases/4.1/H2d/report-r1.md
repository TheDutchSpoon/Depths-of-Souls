## Content changes

- **Pollen Cloud** (Wit, AoE, enemy-side, biome 1): Sleep for 2 turns on every enemy and nothing else; it no longer
  hits for 35% of Intelligence. Still the same gem (a caster may still roll it).
- **Snapback** (Snapjaws' Jaws): `on-damage-taken` → counter at **60%** of Attack as indirect damage (30% in H2c).
- **Poison** 20 → **40%** of the applier's Attack; **Burn** 25 → **35%** of its Intelligence; **Spore** 15 →
  **35%** of its Speed; **Regen** stays 10% of its Health. Every spell and trait that applies them is
  unchanged (they name the status, not the number) and hits harder or softer through the status only.
- `data/statuses.ts` (header and the four notes) and the Pollen Cloud / Snapback doc comments lose "placeholder".
